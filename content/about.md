I'm Arnob Kumar Saha, a platform and DevOps engineer and a team lead at
[AppsCode](https://appscode.com). Most of my work lives at the layer where
Kubernetes stops being a tutorial and starts being an on-call rotation:
operators that run stateful workloads, backup and restore paths that have to
hold under real data, and delivery pipelines that a team can trust.

**What I work with**

- **Go** — operators, controllers, CLI tooling.
- **Kubernetes** — CRDs and controllers, `kubectl`, Helm, Flux-driven GitOps.
- **Databases on Kubernetes** — MongoDB in particular: provisioning, backup,
  restore, and the failure modes that only show up in production.
- **CI/CD and release engineering** — build, tag, and release automation across
  many repositories.
- **Bash** — the glue that holds the rest of it together.

**What I write about here**

Field notes rather than tutorials: what broke, why it broke, and what the fix
actually was. If a post saves one person an afternoon of debugging, it earned
its place.
